How to Use ReadEase

  • Paste Text: Copy and paste any text into the input area above.
  • Adjust Size: Use the + and − buttons to make text larger or smaller.
  • Read Aloud: Click the "Read Aloud" button to hear the text spoken.
  • Simplify: Click "Simplify Text" to rewrite complex sentences into simpler language.
  • Dark Mode: Toggle dark mode for comfortable reading at any time of day.
